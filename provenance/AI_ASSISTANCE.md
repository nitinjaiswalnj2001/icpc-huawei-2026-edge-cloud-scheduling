# AI-Assisted Development Disclosure

The ICPC 2026 Online Challenge 1 powered by Huawei announcement stated that AI tools, including generative AI and AI-assisted coding tools, were permitted during the challenge, with participants remaining responsible for correctness, originality, and rule compliance.

AI-assisted tools were used during this project for activities including:

- brainstorming scheduling hypotheses;
- generating and modifying experimental C++ variants;
- comparing public-judge metrics and identifying likely control surfaces;
- organizing the post-contest experiment ledger;
- drafting and polishing technical documentation and the long-form report.

The contest participant, Nitin Jaiswal, remained responsible for:

- choosing which variants to submit;
- making submissions through the contest account;
- supplying and checking the official public-judge results;
- deciding which variants were banked or rejected;
- preserving the final source and result evidence;
- the public claims made in this repository.

For that reason, generated explanations are not treated as evidence. The repository's source of truth is the preserved code, public result vectors, hashes, and experiment records.
